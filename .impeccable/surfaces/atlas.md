# Atlas surface

Mode: Operate. Existing app redesigned in place; user confirmed preservation of features and data. Code-first because this is an app screen. No standing build-path preference is stored.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A radial index of cybersecurity briefs, where each visible mark corresponds to a real problem and links show actual category overlaps.

OWN-WORLD: User-pinned Hexbrain reference: warm black #14130f, parchment #e9e2cf, muted olive-brown, orange #ff784c only for selected and interactive states. Self-hosted Recursive, small standard controls, quiet rules, hexagonal anchors.

STORY: Scan category sizes and relationships, open a category, read its problem index, then inspect an original brief. Search and filters remain available without taking over the overview.

FIRST VIEWPORT: View switch at top left, centered wordmark, search and filter controls at right. Radial map owns the central workspace. Product introduction at lower left, factual dataset counts at lower right. Category selection moves, zooms, and rotates the camera within the same world and adds a right-hand index. All categories and connections remain rendered; connected lines may continue off-screen. Mobile gets a fit-to-width map and readable category directory.

FORM: Persistent radial world geometry with camera-based focus and category-hover link emphasis. User explicitly rejected isolated category replacement on 2026-11-16. Pan, zoom, rotation, and reset must preserve every graph path and category node. In a focused category, wheel input selects next/previous category with anchored wheel center and focus slot; Ctrl+wheel zooms. Navigation preserves zoom/pan and every connection. Cursor-aligned XY axes span the full visible map, rather than the SVG viewBox or circle. User-supplied screenshots pin the direction. seed: none (user-pinned reference).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
